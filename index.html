The Gospel

There is a question every person must answer: Where will you spend eternity?

The Bible says, "For all have sinned, and come short of the glory of God." (Romans 3:23). The wages of sin is death — but the gift of God is eternal life through Jesus Christ our Lord.

"For God so loved the world, that he gave his only begotten Son, that whosoever believeth in him should not perish, but have everlasting life." — John 3:16, KJV

Jesus Christ died in your place, and rose again. There is nothing to earn. Only to receive.

Pray this from your heart:

"Lord Jesus Christ, I am a sinner. I believe You died for me and rose again. I repent. I turn from my sin. Come into my life. Be my Lord and my Saviour. Amen."

If you prayed that, and you meant it — the Word of God says you are saved. Find a Bible-believing church. Read the Gospel of John. And never let this moment go.
